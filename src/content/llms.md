## Audience Personas

An interactive US audience tool backed only by published research. 8,508 observations across 65 metrics and 29 population segments, drawn from 8 source documents, most recently fielded 2025. Every figure is labelled measured (a published cell, cited) or estimated (combined from published cells in odds space, with the arithmetic printed), and traits that no source measures are shown as gaps rather than filled in. Sample sizes and margins of error appear on every row; the tool draws the margin of error as a hundred dots so the uncertainty is visible rather than stated. Confidence falls as a persona narrows, per Chapman et al. (2008), instead of rising.

- URL: https://venkatapagadala.com/personas
- Data and full crosstabs: https://venkatapagadala.com/personas/data

### Persona Fanout Journey

A universal planning tool covering the entire buyer's journey. One starting query is fanned out by persona scenario and by every stage, Discover, Explore, Compare, Decide and Own, into specific questions; each question states which explicit inputs produced it (budget cap, primary need, hard constraint, timing, location, experience as context only), the content format that tends to answer it, and how to validate it. Works for any purchase: buying a car, buying a phone and choosing a phone plan ship as presets and the product label can be set to anything. Everything it generates is an illustrative hypothesis, not measured demand, and it says so; it is linked to Audience Personas and deliberately kept apart from it.
- URL: https://venkatapagadala.com/personas/fanout-journey

### Persona Builder

A tool that asks the Global Persona Framework's own follow-up questions along a starter path (consumer purchase, B2B buyer, audience), records an evidence status on every answer (self-reported, observed with permission, measured aggregate, derived, estimated, hypothesis, unknown), and composes a persona brief in the framework's P460 format: only what was stated, no invented name or biography. One click opens the persona in the Persona Fanout Journey with its inputs pre-filled. Exports the brief, JSON, an interview guide of unanswered questions, and a P463 validation plan.
- URL: https://venkatapagadala.com/personas/builder

### Global Persona Framework

A classification library of 463 persona dimensions in 42 families: people, households, interests, resources, behaviours and buying contexts. Each dimension carries its definition and global rule, the follow-up question that turns it into an input, the scope and evidence that would support a value, and its handling requirement; 148 reference a named source (UN Statistics, ICO, W3C, ITU, Pew) and the rest are authored with no population estimate attached. It is the vocabulary behind the Persona Fanout Journey. Not a measurement of anyone.
- URL: https://venkatapagadala.com/personas/framework
- Markdown edition: https://venkatapagadala.com/personas/framework.md
- Workbook: https://venkatapagadala.com/personas/framework/Global_Persona_Classification_Framework.xlsx
